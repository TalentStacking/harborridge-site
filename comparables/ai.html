Methodology

Universe. Private M&A transactions of US-based AI-native companies, plus non-AI companies (developer tools, media, power and others) bought by AI-native companies such as model labs and AI clouds, shown in their own Non-AI Targets segment (AI applications and agents, model platforms, AI infrastructure and developer tools) announced in 2025 and 2026, including deals where the price was not disclosed. Deals without disclosed revenue or EBITDA are shown without a multiple, and deals without a price show no enterprise value; both are left out of the medians and averages.

Multiples. Enterprise value divided by trailing revenue (EV / Rev) and EBITDA (EV / EBITDA). Where a deal was reported on annual recurring revenue or on current-year or next-year estimates, the basis is noted in the rationale. All deals with a multiple are included in the medians.

Dates. Where only the month of a transaction is known, the date is shown as the first of that month.

Buyer type. Strategic: corporate acquirers. Private equity: sponsor take-privates and platform investments. PE-backed strategic: acquisitions by sponsor-owned companies.

Public Comps Methodology

Universe. US-listed AI-native companies covered by our live price feed, grouped into two segments: AI Platforms & Infrastructure (Palantir, CoreWeave, C3.ai, BigBear.ai) and AI Applications (UiPath, SoundHound AI, Tempus AI). Software companies adding AI features are covered on the Software page.

Multiples. Enterprise value divided by LTM revenue and LTM EBITDA, in US$ millions, as of November 18, 2026. Click Refresh to pull current share prices and market caps for all 7 companies; EV is recalculated holding net debt constant, and the multiples are recomputed against the same LTM financials. EV / EBITDA is shown as n/m where EBITDA is negative or the multiple exceeds 100x.

EV / Revenue over time. Each quarterly point uses that quarter-end closing price, diluted weighted-average shares, net debt (total debt less cash and short-term investments) and LTM revenue from SEC filings for the latest fiscal quarter ended on or before that date, for all 7 companies (CoreWeave from Q1 2025, after its IPO). The final point is today's live value.

Growth and Rule of 40. Growth is LTM revenue growth year over year. Rule of 40 is growth plus LTM EBITDA margin.
