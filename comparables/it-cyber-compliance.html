Methodology & Sources

Universe. Private M&A transactions in IT and cyber compliance: compliance audit and assessment firms (3PAOs, C3PAOs, SOC and ISO auditors), compliance and GRC software, cyber and GRC advisory services, CMMC and FedRAMP specialists, and testing, inspection, certification and compliance (TICC) platforms. US and Europe, 2022 onward.

Multiples. Enterprise value divided by last twelve months EBITDA (EV / LTM EBITDA). Most compliance deals do not disclose terms; multiples shown are Harbor Ridge Capital estimates derived from publicly available information and market intelligence, and are directionally correct but not independently verified. Qmulos is shown at 10x ARR (EV / Rev column). Where several deals were reported at or above 25x, they are shown at 25.0x.

Dates. Where only the month of a transaction is known, the date is shown as the first of that month.

Sources. PitchBook, press releases, and Harbor Ridge Capital research, including the HRC CMMC and FedRAMP 20x whitepapers (Q2 2026).

Public Comps Methodology

Universe. Two groups of publicly traded companies most relevant to compliance buyers: global TICC majors (SGS, Eurofins, Bureau Veritas, Intertek) and government IT and managed services firms (Leidos, CACI, Parsons, SAIC, Kratos).

Multiples. Enterprise value divided by LTM revenue and LTM EBITDA, in US$ millions, as of June 10, 2026. Kratos (99.3x EV / EBITDA) is shown in the table but excluded from the averages and medians as an outlier.

Rule of 40. Revenue growth plus EBITDA margin.

Sources. Capital IQ, company filings, and Harbor Ridge Capital research (HRC FedRAMP 20x whitepaper, Q2 2026).
