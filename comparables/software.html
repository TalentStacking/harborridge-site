Methodology

Universe. Private M&A transactions and take-privates of US-based software companies announced in 2025 and 2026, limited to deals where an enterprise value multiple of revenue, recurring revenue or EBITDA was disclosed or can be derived from disclosed figures. Deals without valuation data are not included.

Multiples. Enterprise value divided by trailing revenue (EV / Rev) and EBITDA (EV / EBITDA). Where a deal was reported on annual recurring revenue or on current-year or next-year estimates, the basis is noted in the rationale. All deals in the table are included in the medians.

Dates. Where only the month of a transaction is known, the date is shown as the first of that month.

Buyer type. Strategic: corporate acquirers. Private equity: sponsor take-privates and platform investments. PE-backed strategic: acquisitions by sponsor-owned companies.

Public Comps Methodology

Universe. US-listed software companies covered by our live price feed, grouped into five segments: Enterprise Applications (Salesforce, ServiceNow, Workday, Adobe, Atlassian), Infrastructure & Data (Snowflake, Datadog, MongoDB, Dynatrace, GitLab), Cybersecurity (CrowdStrike, Zscaler, Okta, Qualys, Rubrik), Vertical Software (Veeva, AppFolio, Procore, ServiceTitan, Waystar) and SMB/Consumer (HubSpot, Shopify, Paycom, Paylocity, Klaviyo). Contact center and communications software is covered on the CCaaS & UCaaS page.

Multiples. Enterprise value divided by LTM revenue and LTM EBITDA, in US$ millions, as of November 17, 2026. Click Refresh to pull current share prices and market caps for all 26 companies; EV is recalculated holding net debt constant, and the multiples are recomputed against the same LTM financials. EV / EBITDA is shown as n/m where EBITDA is negative or the multiple exceeds 100x.

EV / Revenue over time. Each quarterly point uses that quarter-end closing price, diluted weighted-average shares, net debt (total debt less cash and short-term investments) and LTM revenue from SEC filings for the latest fiscal quarter ended on or before that date, for all 25 companies. The final point is today's live value.

Growth and Rule of 40. Growth is LTM revenue growth year over year. Rule of 40 is growth plus LTM EBITDA margin.

Figures as of November 17, 2026. Refresh updates all 26 companies.
