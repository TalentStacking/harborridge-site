Methodology & Sources

Universe. Private M&A and growth investments in emerging technology services: AI implementation and forward-deployed engineering, data and analytics consultancies, cloud and SaaS platform partners (Databricks, Snowflake, Salesforce, SAP, Oracle, Google Cloud, Microsoft, Palantir), digital and product engineering, and technology-enabled IT consulting and managed services. US-based targets only, 2025 onward.

Deal terms. Most technology services deals do not disclose terms. Enterprise value is shown only where a value was reported, using the upfront or initial enterprise value (earnouts noted in the rationale) and converted to US dollars where needed. EV / Revenue and EV / EBITDA are shown only where the buyer or seller disclosed the target's revenue or EBITDA (trailing, or forward where noted). Medians exclude any outliers marked in the rationale. Growth and minority investments are noted in the rationale.

Buyer type. Strategic: corporate and consulting acquirers. Private equity: sponsor platform or growth investments. PE-backed strategic: add-ons by sponsor-owned platforms. AI lab: OpenAI's Deployment Company and Ode (Anthropic, Blackstone and Hellman & Friedman).

Sources. Press releases, company announcements, and Harbor Ridge Capital research, including the HRC insight The Picks-and-Shovels Play in AI Is Services.

Public Comps Methodology

Universe. US-listed technology services companies covered by our live price feed, combining digital engineering and large IT and BPO services firms into one set: Accenture, Cognizant, Genpact, EPAM, Globant and Grid Dynamics. Companies that report in other currencies (such as CGI, Wipro, CI&T and Endava) are not included.

Multiples. Enterprise value divided by LTM revenue and LTM EBITDA, in US$ millions, from Capital IQ as of September 30, 2026. Growth and margin are 2026 consensus estimates. Click Refresh to pull current share prices and market caps from Finnhub for all six; EV is recalculated using net debt implied at September 30, and EV / Rev and EV / EBITDA are recomputed against the same LTM financials.

Rule of 40. 2026E revenue growth plus 2026E EBITDA margin.

EV / EBITDA over time. Each quarterly point uses that quarter-end closing price, diluted weighted-average shares, and net debt (total debt less cash and short-term investments) and LTM operating income from SEC filings for the latest fiscal quarter ended on or before that date, for Accenture, Cognizant, Genpact and EPAM. To keep the table's EBITDA definitions, each company's LTM EBITDA is rolled back from the table figure by the change in reported LTM operating income. Globant (no quarterly SEC financials) and Grid Dynamics (near-zero operating income makes the roll-back unreliable) are left out of the chart. The final point is today's live value.

Sources. Capital IQ, company filings, Finnhub (live prices), and Harbor Ridge Capital research. Historical closing prices from public market data; SEC EDGAR filings.

Figures as of September 30, 2026. Refresh updates all six companies.
