Cyber & Compliance Trends — The CMMC Economy

Whitepaper · Industry Insights

Continuing to leverage our experience from closing six transactions across cyber and compliance, we are pleased to share our latest report on the Compliance SaaS & Services market.

With CMMC 2.0 now contractually mandated for the ~300,000 vendors in the Defense Industrial Base, 2026 marks a critical transition year for the ecosystem. Our latest research provides a deep dive into the "CMMC Economy" with the following:

  • Regulatory Roadmap and Deadlines — the latest as of January 2026
  • Market Size and Growth — estimated market size and growth by segment
  • M&A & Financing Activity — deeper dive into recent transaction activity
  • Strategic Acquirer Profiles — geared around the most acquisitive players in the space and their private-equity sponsors
  • Market Map — ~500 leading companies across the CMMC ecosystem
  • Public Comparables — the most relevant public companies across cyber, compliance, and govcon
