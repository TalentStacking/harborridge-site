The FedRAMP 20x Inflection

Whitepaper · Industry Insights

Building on our recent experience advising leading providers across the cyber and compliance ecosystem — including the sale of an ISO/CMMC compliance SaaS platform to LRQA, a Goldman Sachs portfolio company — we are pleased to share our latest report on the FedRAMP market.

With FedRAMP 20x set to become the default authorization path in Q3 FY2026, the federal cloud compliance market is entering a structural inflection. A faster, automation-first model is expected to more than double the assessment and advisory market by 2033, while strategic acquirers and sponsors are already consolidating ahead of the wave. Our latest research provides a deep dive into the "FedRAMP 20x Economy" with the following:

  • FedRAMP 20x Overview — before and after, timeline, and the new ecosystem
  • Market Size and Growth — estimated market size and growth by segment
  • M&A & Financing Activity — deeper dive into recent transaction activity
  • Strategic Acquirer Profiles — geared around the most acquisitive players in the space and their private-equity sponsors
  • FedRAMP Economy Market Map — ~90 leading companies across the ecosystem
  • Public Comparables — the most relevant public companies across cyber, compliance, and govcon
